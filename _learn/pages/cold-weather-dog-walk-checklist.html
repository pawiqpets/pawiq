How cold is too cold to walk a dog?

There is no single number for every dog. Below freezing, most dogs, especially thin-coated ones, should not stay out more than 10 to 15 minutes without a warmer space, and puppies and short-haired pets should come inside below 40°F (about 4°C).1, 2

What are the signs a dog is too cold?

Shivering, whining, anxiety, slowing down or stopping and weakness are signs of hypothermia, so get your dog back inside quickly if you see them.3, 4

Talk to your vet. If you suspect hypothermia or frostbite, or notice concerning signs of a cold-related injury, call your vet right away. Your vet can also advise on coats and paw care for your dog.
