Last updated November 6, 2026

Free, no sign-up. Calculators and printable checklists built from veterinary and AAFCO guidance.

Calculators

  • Dog age calculator: an estimate of your dog's age in human years, from a 2020 study.
  • Dog supplement cost calculator: cost per day and per month from the package price and label serving.

Printable checklists

  • Dog boarding checklist: from choosing a facility to pickup day.
  • Cold weather dog walk checklist: before, during and after a winter walk.

For the reasons behind the checklists, browse the Learn guides.

Every tool lists the sources behind it, gives no dosing or medical advice, and sends your numbers nowhere. Questions about your own dog belong with your vet.
