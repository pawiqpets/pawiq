Dog age calculator.

Enter your dog's age to see a research-based estimate of the human age that matches it. It takes a few seconds and stores nothing.

How does the estimate work?

The estimate uses a formula from a 2020 study in the journal Cell Systems, which tracked chemical changes in DNA as 104 Labrador retrievers aged and compared them with human patterns: human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31, with dog age in years.1 It replaces the old "multiply by 7" rule, because dogs age fastest early in life and more slowly later.1

How accurate is it for my breed?

It was built on one breed, Labrador retrievers, so treat the result as a fun, rough guide for any dog.1 Dogs of different sizes and breeds have different lifespans, so the match will not be exact for every dog. It is not medical advice, and it says nothing about your dog's health.

What should I use this for?

Use it as a conversation starter, or to picture where your dog is in life. For anything about your dog's care at a given age, ask your vet.
