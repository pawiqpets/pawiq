Dog supplement cost calculator.

Work out what any chew, treat or supplement really costs per day and per month. Use the numbers from the package, and the serving size printed on its label.

How is the cost worked out?

Cost per serving is the package price divided by the number of servings in it. Cost per day is the cost per serving times the servings you give each day. A month is counted as 30 days. Nothing leaves your device.

Where do I find the serving size?

The serving size is on the product's label. The label is the right place to take it from, so use the number printed there for your dog, or ask your vet. This calculator does not suggest an amount for any dog.

What else should I compare?

Price per day is one piece. Compare the ingredient list and what each serving contains too. Our guide to reading a supplement label walks through it part by part.
