How we write Learn.

Learn is published by pawIQ, which makes daily soft chews for dogs. Our guides cover everyday dog care in plain language.

Where our facts come from

Every fact in a Learn guide comes from a source you can check, such as veterinary schools, the Merck Veterinary Manual, the AVMA, the FDA, AAFCO and peer-reviewed research. We link each source so you can read it yourself.

How we use AI

We use AI tools to help research and draft. Every guide is checked against its sources before it goes live.

Keeping guides current

We review guides regularly and update them when guidance changes. The "Last reviewed" date on each guide shows when.

Not veterinary advice

Learn guides are general information. For anything about your own dog's health, talk to your vet.
